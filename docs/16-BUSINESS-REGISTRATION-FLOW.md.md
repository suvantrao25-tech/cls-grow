Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Last Updated: 31 July 2026

---

# Objective

Register a business in less than 5 minutes.

The process should be simple, fast, and require only essential information.

---

# Step 1 - Create Account

Required Fields

- Full Name
- Mobile Number
- Email Address
- Password

System Action

- Verify Email
- Create User Profile

---

# Step 2 - Register Business

Required Fields

- Business Name
- Business Category
- Business Type
- Owner Name
- Mobile Number

Optional Fields

- GST Number
- Website
- Google Business Profile
- Social Media Links

---

# Step 3 - Business Location

Required Fields

- Address
- City
- State
- PIN Code
- Country

Optional

- Google Maps Location

---

# Step 4 - Business Details

Required

- Years in Business
- Number of Employees
- Products / Services
- Business Goal

Examples of Goals

- Increase Sales
- Get More Customers
- Improve Online Presence
- Increase Repeat Customers

---

# Step 5 - AI Setup

CLS Grow automatically:

- Creates Business Profile
- Calculates Initial Business Score
- Calculates Initial Growth Score
- Generates First AI Report
- Detects Initial Opportunities

No manual work required.

---

# Step 6 - Dashboard

Business owner is redirected to Dashboard.

Dashboard displays:

- Business Score
- Growth Score
- AI Recommendations
- Today's Opportunities
- Latest Report
- Notifications

---

# Success Criteria

Business registration should take less than 5 minutes.

Business owner should understand the dashboard within 1 minute.
