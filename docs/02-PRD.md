# CLS Grow - Product Requirement Document (PRD) v1.0

## Product Overview

CLS Grow is an AI-powered Business Growth Platform designed to help local businesses grow with minimal effort from the business owner.

---

# Problem

Small businesses are busy running their daily operations.

Most don't have time or knowledge to use modern technology for growth.

---

# Solution

CLS Grow continuously analyzes the business and provides AI-powered growth insights, automation, reports, and recommendations.

Business owner focuses on business.

CLS Grow focuses on business growth.

- Spend less than 10 minutes per week inside CLS Grow.
- Receive clear business insights.
- Feel the subscription delivers real value every month.
