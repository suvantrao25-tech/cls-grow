# Customer Journey

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Last Updated: 31 July 2026

---

# Objective

Design the complete journey of a business owner from signup to business growth.

CLS Grow should require minimal effort from the business owner while delivering continuous value.

---

# Stage 1 — Discovery

Business owner discovers CLS Grow through:

- Google Search
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube
- Referral
- Creator Launch Space

Goal

Understand what CLS Grow does in less than 60 seconds.

---

# Stage 2 — Registration

Business owner creates an account.

Required Information

- Name
- Mobile Number
- Email
- Password

Time Target

Less than 2 minutes.

---

# Stage 3 — Business Registration

Business owner registers the business.

Information Required

- Business Name
- Business Category
- Address
- City
- State
- Google Business Profile (Optional)
- Website (Optional)

Time Target

Less than 5 minutes.

---

# Stage 4 — AI Business Analysis

CLS Grow automatically starts analyzing the business.

AI Checks

- Profile Completeness
- Online Presence
- Business Category
- Missing Information
- Initial Growth Opportunities

Business owner only waits.

No manual work required.

---

# Stage 5 — Dashboard

Business owner sees:

- Business Score
- Growth Score
- AI Recommendations
- Today's Opportunities
- Reports
- Notifications

Everything should be understandable within one minute.

---

# Stage 6 — Continuous Growth

CLS Grow works continuously.

Daily

- AI Monitoring
- Opportunity Detection

Weekly

- Growth Review
- AI Suggestions

Monthly

- Business Report
- Growth Report
- Business Score Update

---

# Stage 7 — Subscription

When the business owner sees value, they can upgrade.

Plans

- Free
- Basic
- Pro
- Enterprise

---

# Success Criteria

Business owner should:

- Spend less than 10 minutes per week using CLS Grow.
- Receive useful AI insights automatically.
- Feel that the subscription is worth paying every month.

---

# CLS Grow Promise

You run your business.

CLS Grow works on your business growth.
