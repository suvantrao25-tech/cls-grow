# Objective

Build the smallest version of CLS Grow that delivers real value to business owners.

The MVP should solve the core problem before adding advanced features.

---

# Included in MVP

## Authentication

- Sign Up
- Login
- Email Verification
- Forgot Password

---

## Business Registration

- Multi-Step Registration
- Business Type
- Business Category
- Business Details

---

## Dashboard

- Business Score
- Growth Score
- AI Recommendations
- Notifications
- Reports Summary

---

## AI

- Business Analysis
- Growth Suggestions
- Priority Actions

---

## Reports

- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly

---

## Subscription

- Free
- Pro

---

# Not Included

- Mobile App
- WhatsApp Automation
- CRM
- Inventory Management
- Accounting
- Employee Management
- POS System
- Multi-language
- Multi-country

---

# Success Criteria

- User can register a business.
- AI generates recommendations.
- Dashboard loads correctly.
- Reports are generated.
- User can upgrade to Pro.

# MVP Target

Launch within 90 days.
