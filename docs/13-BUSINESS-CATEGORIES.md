Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

## Purpose

CLS Grow is designed to support multiple business categories.

Each business category has unique challenges, customers, operations, and growth strategies.

The AI Engine will provide industry-specific recommendations and automation based on the selected business category.

---

# Category 1 – Retail

Examples

- Kirana Store
- Medical Store
- Clothing Shop
- Mobile Shop
- Electronics Shop
- Hardware Store
- Stationery Shop
- Gift Shop

Primary Goals

- Increase daily sales
- Increase repeat customers
- Improve local visibility
- Build customer loyalty
