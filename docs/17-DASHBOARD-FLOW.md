# Objective

Provide a simple, actionable, and AI-powered dashboard that helps business owners understand their business at a glance.

The dashboard should answer three questions:

1. How is my business performing?
2. What should I do today?
3. How can I grow?

---

# Dashboard Sections

## 1. Welcome

- Business Name
- Owner Name
- Greeting
- Current Plan

---

## 2. Business Health

- Business Score
- Growth Score
- Health Status

---

## 3. Today's Opportunities

Examples:

- Complete Google Business Profile
- Reply to Customer Reviews
- Update Business Hours
- Publish New Offer

---

## 4. AI Recommendations

Priority actions generated by CLS Grow AI.

Examples:

- Improve Google Reviews
- Add Product Photos
- Optimize Business Description
- Launch Local Promotion

---

## 5. Reports

- Weekly Report
- Monthly Report
- Growth Report

---

## 6. Notifications

- AI Alerts
- Business Reminders
- Growth Updates

---

## 7. Quick Actions

- Update Business Profile
- Generate AI Report
- View Growth Plan
- Contact Support

---

# Dashboard Goals

- Easy to understand
- Mobile Friendly
- Action-Oriented
- Less than 1 minute to understand
