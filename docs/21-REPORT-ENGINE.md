# Report Engine

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

# Objective

Generate AI-powered business reports that help business owners understand
their current performance, identify opportunities, and take action.

Reports should be simple, visual, and actionable.

---

# Report Types

## Daily Report

Purpose

Provide a quick overview of today's business status.

Contents

- Business Score
- Growth Score
- AI Recommendations
- Today's Opportunities
- Notifications

---

## Weekly Report

Purpose

Summarize business activities and progress.

Contents

- Business Score Changes
- Growth Score Changes
- Completed AI Tasks
- Pending Tasks
- Weekly Achievements
- Improvement Areas

---

## Monthly Report

Purpose

Provide a complete business performance review.

Contents

- Monthly Business Summary
- Business Score Trend
- Growth Score Trend
- AI Recommendations Completed
- Opportunities Identified
- Improvements Made
- Goals Achieved
- Goals Pending

---

## Quarterly Report (Future)

Purpose

Analyze long-term business growth.

Contents

- Quarterly Performance
- Growth Trends
- Major Achievements
- Strategic Recommendations

---

## Annual Report (Future)

Purpose

Review yearly business performance.

Contents

- Yearly Summary
- Business Growth Timeline
- Business Score History
- Growth Score History
- Key Milestones
- AI Insights
- Future Growth Plan
