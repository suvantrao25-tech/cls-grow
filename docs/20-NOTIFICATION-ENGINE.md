# Notification Engine

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Last Updated: 31 July 2026

---

# Objective

Deliver timely, relevant, and actionable notifications that help business owners grow their business.

CLS Grow should notify only when there is value for the business owner.

---

# Notification Channels

- In-App Notifications
- Email
- WhatsApp (Future)
- Push Notifications (Future)

---

# Notification Categories

## AI Recommendations

Examples

- New growth opportunity detected.
- High-priority recommendation available.
- Your monthly growth plan is ready.

---

## Business Alerts

Examples

- Business profile is incomplete.
- Google Business Profile needs attention.
- Website information is outdated.

---

## Reports

Examples

- Weekly Growth Report is ready.
- Monthly Business Report is available.
- Business Score has been updated.

---

## Subscription

Examples

- Free plan limit reached.
- Subscription renewal reminder.
- Payment successful.
- Payment failed.

---

## System Notifications

Examples

- Welcome to CLS Grow.
- New feature available.
- Scheduled maintenance.

---

# Notification Priority

High

- Business-critical issues
- Payment failures
- Security alerts

Medium

- AI recommendations
- Weekly reports
- Growth opportunities

Low

- Product updates
- Feature announcements
- Tips and tutorials

---

# Notification Rules

- Never send duplicate notifications.
- Keep messages short and actionable.
- Allow users to manage notification preferences.
- Respect user time and avoid unnecessary alerts.

---

# Notification Settings

Users can enable or disable:

- Email Notifications
- In-App Notifications
- Weekly Reports
- Monthly Reports
- Marketing Updates

---

# Success Metrics

- High notification open rate.
- High action completion rate.
- Low notification unsubscribe rate.
